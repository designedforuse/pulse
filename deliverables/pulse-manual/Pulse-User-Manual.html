Quick start

Three destinations, one place to begin.

  1. Use the bottom tabs: Watch (pulse icon), Rituals (shirt icon), and Stories (stars icon).
  2. Start in Watch for current and next events. Tap a card for its details and viewing options.
  3. Open a gear to reach Settings; choose favorites and viewing preferences there. You can return to Watch, Rituals, or Stories using the bottom tabs.
Pulse helps you discover and navigate events. To watch a stream, you need your own access to the relevant provider or service; video does not play inside Pulse.
02 /

Watch

Find the live moment, then look ahead.

  1. Tap the pulse tab. Browse current and next events; scroll for more cards and sections.
  2. See Chaos Mode for a ranked, compelling live pick when eligible events are available. It is a discovery signal, not a guarantee of coverage.
  3. Tap an event card for details and available viewing links. An upcoming event shows a disabled Starting soon action; a live event may show Join Live; a finished event may show Watch Replay.
Availability varies. A button or provider listing does not guarantee that a stream or replay is accessible. Provider subscriptions, location, rights, and replay availability may apply.

Rituals

A weekend guide organized by viewing mood.

  1. Tap the shirt tab to open Rituals.
  2. Swipe through the ritual carousel to browse each themed window. Tap a ritual card to open its guide and featured events.

Stories

The day’s narratives, together.

  1. Tap the stars tab to open Stories.
  2. Tap The Daily Wrap card to open the full wrap and read its stories.
  3. To control final-score spoilers in the wrap, open Settings and adjust Hide scores in Daily Wrap.
Spoiler scope: hiding scores in the Daily Wrap does not hide scores across every live or event screen. Treat live scores elsewhere in Pulse as visible.
05 /

Settings & favorites

Make discovery fit what you follow.

  1. Tap a gear to open Settings. Expand Your Favorites to manage favorites.
  2. Use Only show favorites in schedules to restrict schedule lists to your favorites. If a schedule looks empty, check this filter first.
  3. Expand Sports Packs and Networks to review those preferences.
  4. Hide scores in Daily Wrap is on by default; turn it off if you want final scores shown on wrap story cards.
Settings groups expand when tapped. The screenshot shows their collapsed state; options such as the favorites-only schedule filter appear inside the expanded group.

My Plan

A small, local viewing plan for Serie A.

  1. On Watch, tap View my plan to open My Plan. This is a Serie A pilot only.
  2. Open a Serie A event and choose Watch live or Highlights to record your viewing intention.
  3. Back in My Plan, switch between This week and Upcoming; use the available filter, change an intention, or remove an item as needed.
Your plan is stored locally on this device; it does not sync across devices. “Highlights” is an intention, not a promise that highlights will exist. No reminders or notifications are promised.

Refresh & troubleshooting

Start with filters, then check the data.

In Settings → Refresh Data, use Refresh Schedules to request schedule data again. Rebuild Stories is a separate action for story content; refreshing schedules does not replace rebuilding stories.

Schedule looks empty?

Check Only show favorites in schedules and your selected preferences. Clear a restrictive filter, then try Refresh Schedules.

Connection or API error?

Check your connection and retry the relevant refresh. If it persists, capture a screenshot showing the exact error message and where it appeared; do not assume the error is caused by a filter.

Story content looks stale?

Use Rebuild Stories under Refresh Data. This is distinct from Refresh Schedules.

Provider link not working?

Check your provider access and availability in your region. A listed stream or replay may be unavailable even when its event card is visible.

Important notes

What the guide does—and does not—promise.

  • Coverage depends on available data. Pulse does not guarantee complete fixtures or live scores for every league, or that every event has a stream, replay, or highlights.
  • Ritual window definitions use Pacific Time. Individual fixture times can be displayed according to your device; check the event detail for the time shown to you.
  • The five screenshots are authentic phone-sized web preview captures from September 29, 2026 (PT). A native build can look different. Dates, scores, counts, and times pictured are illustrative snapshots, not live schedule information.
  • This manual describes discovery and navigation. It does not promise in-app video playback, reminders, notifications, or cross-device plan synchronization.
