## Mid-Season Break & National Youth Weeks
Following the mid-June fixtures, local Western Cape league games pause for the winter holidays and the SA Rugby Youth Weeks. Western Cape provincial school teams (Western Province and Boland) will compete on the national stage during these dates: [5, 6] 

* U16 Grant Khomo Week: 29 June – 3 July 2026 at Kearsney College, Durban.
* U18 Craven Week & Academy Week: 6 July – 11 July 2026 at Grey High School, Gqeberha. [5, 6]
